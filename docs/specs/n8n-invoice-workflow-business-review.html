Invoice Automation

Purpose: We'd like your feedback on whether this workflow matches how invoices are actually handled today, and whether we're missing anything.

What this is: A plan to automate invoice processing. Instead of manually reading invoices, categorizing them, and entering them into QuickBooks, most of that would happen automatically. You'd just review and approve via text message.

How It Would Work

1 Invoices arrive by email

Vendors send invoices (PDF or photo) to a dedicated email address (e.g. ap@yourdomain.com). This inbox is only for invoices — nothing else.

↓

2 AI reads the invoice

The system automatically reads the invoice and pulls out:

  • Vendor name (who sent it)
  • Invoice number
  • Date and due date
  • Total amount
  • Line items (what was purchased)
  • Expense category (matched against your existing QuickBooks categories)

This works with both regular PDFs and photos/scans of paper invoices.

↓

3 You get a text message for approval

You'll receive a text like:

New invoice from ACME Supplies
Amount: $1,250.00
Date: 04/01/2026 / Due: 04/30/2026
Category: Office Supplies

Reply YES to approve, NO to reject
↓

4 You reply, and it's handled

If you reply YES: The expense is automatically logged in QuickBooks with the vendor name, amount, date, category, and invoice number. You get a confirmation text.

If you reply NO: The invoice is not logged. You get a confirmation text and can handle it manually.

What We Need From You

1. Does this match your current process?

Are we missing any steps between receiving an invoice and logging it in QuickBooks?

2. Expense categories

Can you share the list of expense categories you currently use in QuickBooks? The system needs this list to categorize invoices correctly.

3. Is the text message enough?

Is the summary above (vendor, amount, date, due date, category) enough to decide whether to approve or reject? Would you want to see anything else?

4. Edge cases — do any of these apply?
  • Do you ever need to split an invoice across multiple categories?
  • Do invoices need to be assigned to a specific job or project?
  • Are there invoices that should be automatically approved (e.g. recurring monthly bills)?
  • Do you sometimes need to adjust the amount or add notes before approving?
  • What happens today if an invoice looks wrong or there's a dispute?
5. Volume

Roughly how many invoices come in per week or month?

What This Doesn't Cover (Yet)

These could be added later if useful:

  • Bill pay / scheduling payments — this only logs the expense, it doesn't pay anything
  • Duplicate detection — flagging if the same invoice comes in twice
  • Monthly reports — summarizing what was approved or rejected
  • Multiple approvers — right now it goes to one phone number
Please reply with any feedback — no detail is too small. If something doesn't match how things actually work, we want to know.
